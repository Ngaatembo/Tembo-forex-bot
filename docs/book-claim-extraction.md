# Book Claim Extraction

This registry separates source-derived concepts from Tembo's operational rules and from profitability conclusions.

## Status meanings
- `EVIDENCE_ONLY`: the concept is visible to analysis but does not authorize a trade.
- `METHODOLOGY_ONLY`: the source informs research design rather than a signal.
- `UNVALIDATED`: no profitability conclusion has been made.

## Promotion rule
A claim becomes a trading hypothesis only after its conditions can be represented deterministically, tested without look-ahead, compared with a baseline, and evaluated on validation and untouched out-of-sample data.

Until then, Tembo should show the claim as evidence or research context only.

## Important distinction
The registry records the source-supported concept and separately records Tembo's numerical operationalization. A threshold invented by the engineering team must never be presented as a quotation or exact rule from the source.
